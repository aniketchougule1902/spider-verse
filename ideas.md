# Spider-Verse Galaxy — Design Brainstorm

## Three possible visual directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Orbital Noir** | A cinematic observatory drifting through a layered Milky Way, with editorial typography and subtle web geometry. It feels like a secret interdimensional research station rather than a game interface. | 0.07 |
| **Bento Multiverse** | A bright, playful collage of portal cards, comic captions, and dimensional artifacts. It favors kinetic layouts and approachable exploration. | 0.04 |
| **Stellar Field Notes** | A refined field-journal for web-slinging astronomers, combining blueprint lines, scientific labels, and a warm cosmic dusk palette. | 0.09 |

## Selected approach: Orbital Noir

### Design Movement

**Cinematic science-fiction editorialism** with restrained Spider-Verse energy. The interface should resemble the launch screen for a deep-space observatory that happens to be guided by a web-slinger.

### Core Principles

1. **Asymmetric storytelling:** large copy and controls anchor the left while celestial instrumentation and orbital objects occupy the right.
2. **Luminous restraint:** use red sparingly as the web-slinger signal against midnight blues and atmospheric nebula whites.
3. **Tactile evidence:** constellation lines, registration marks, lenses, and faint grain make the experience feel collected rather than generic.
4. **Purposeful motion:** all movement suggests orbit, signal scanning, drifting dust, or a web being flung—not random decoration.

### Color Philosophy

The background is almost-black **indigo space** so pale text, stars, and the long-exposure Milky Way can glow clearly. A near-white moonlit hue supports the requested plain Spider-Man image, while **arachnid red** functions as an alert color for actions, web traces, and navigational state. Cool cyan appears only as an instrument-reading highlight.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is constructed as a **vertical orbital journey**, not a centered-card website. The signed-in state opens to an off-center hero with a left editorial rail and a right “observatory” scene; later sections alternate between offset records and directional scroll cues.

### Signature Elements

1. A thin **web reticle** that acts as the cursor and expands into a thrown web on click.
2. A **white-line antenna / telescope instrument cluster** over the hero visual.
3. Small scientific **coordinate labels and registration corners** that mark sections, controls, and facts.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactive items feel like satellite controls: compact, precise, and clear. The web cursor makes the physical action special, while menus and links stay accessible as normal buttons and anchors.

### Animation

Stars twinkle softly, the Milky Way breathes in slow opacity shifts, the asteroid tracks a gentle diagonal orbit, and cosmic dust moves on lengthy linear loops. Interface entrances use 180–360ms opacity/translate transitions; the connection splash is a single dramatic but short 1.6-second dot-field expansion. All motion reduces for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is the display face for tense, compressed headings and measurement labels. **Manrope** carries utility copy and readable paragraphs. Headings use high contrast weights with controlled letterspacing; utility labels use uppercase small caps with deliberate tracking.

### Brand Essence

**Spider-Verse Galaxy is an interdimensional observatory for fans who want to trace Spider-heroes through real cosmic wonder.**

Personality: **cinematic, curious, precise**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are observational and charged; CTAs are short operational prompts; microcopy is crisp and technical without sounding cold.

> “Your universe just gained a signal.”

> “Trace the next anomaly.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a **red orbital arc pinched into a spider abdomen**, with four hairline legs that double as telescope calibration marks. The name is set as a custom split wordmark: `SPIDER / VERSE` stacked over a tracked `GALAXY` descriptor.

### Signature Brand Color

**Arachnid Signal Red — `#E43838`**. It is used only where momentum, direction, or a web-slinger action must be unmistakable.

## Style Decisions

- Character imagery is treated as a moonlit observatory scan with calibration geometry and cosmic integration; it is never presented as a flat studio cutout.
- The hero’s right side must always contain visible white-line instrument drawings, coordinates, and a tracking reticle before it reads as a conventional photo panel.
- The split wordmark and red orbital-spider mark are primary identity assets, used at a clearly legible scale rather than as minor decoration.
